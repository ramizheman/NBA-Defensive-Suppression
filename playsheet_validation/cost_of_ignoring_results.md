# Cost of continuing a suppressed play

When a suppression event fires and the offense keeps running the play (≥3 second-half attempts, n=683), PPP on that play type is 0.876 versus a 1.522 first-half baseline. The collapse is already present on the first five second-half attempts (0.852).
